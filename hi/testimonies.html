Testimonies

Hope isn't just something we talk about on Sundays. Here's how it's shown up in the everyday lives of people in our church.

Restored

"I found a family here."

After moving to Kathmandu for work, I walked into a Sunday Gathering knowing no one. Two years on, this church is home — people showed up for me before I even asked.

— Sabina T.

Freed

"Hope replaced my fear."

I was carrying a lot of anxiety about the future. The Youth Night community walked with me through a hard season and kept pointing me back to Christ instead of pretending everything was fine.

— Anish K.

Renewed

"A genuine fresh start."

I came for a Community Service Day and stayed because people prayed for my family without being asked twice. That kind of care is rare, and it's real here.

— Prakriti M.

Healed

"I stopped carrying it alone."

My Home Group became the first place I could be honest about my grief. No one rushed me to feel better — they just stayed.

— Deepak S.

Grounded

"Scripture finally made sense."

I'd read the Bible before but never understood it. Sunday teaching here, paired with real conversations, changed that completely.

— Menuka R.

Welcomed

"No one made me feel behind."

I'm still asking a lot of questions about faith. What I love is that nobody here has made me feel like that's a problem.

— Bikash G.

Your story could be next. Come see for yourself this Sunday.
