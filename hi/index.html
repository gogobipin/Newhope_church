Welcome to New Hope Church

A community in Kathmandu anchored in one unshakeable hope — that in Christ, every broken thing can be made new. Come as you are.

Join us in person→ Join in online

"If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation."

2 Corinthians 5:17

At New Hope Church, our identity rests in a simple truth: real hope isn't an optimistic wish, it's a living person — Jesus Christ. In a season of hardship and uncertainty across Nepal, the gospel offers an anchor for the soul.

We believe that through the cross and resurrection, Christ breaks every chain, restores what was broken, and gives every person a genuine fresh start. That redemptive hope is the heartbeat of our ministry — and our promise to Nepal.
