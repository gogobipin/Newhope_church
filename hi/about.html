Hope isn't a feeling we chase. It's a person we follow.

We are a community anchored in one unshakeable hope, experiencing the presence of God and partnering with Jesus to bring His peace into everyday life.

Who we are

At the heart of our gatherings is a passionate pursuit of God's presence — where hearts are transformed, grace is revealed, and believers are launched into their calling. We're a mixed congregation of long-time Kathmandu families and newcomers to the city, meeting weekly to worship, learn, and care for one another.

Whether you grew up in church or you're walking through the door for the first time, you'll find people glad you came.

Our beliefs

Our convictions are drawn from Scripture and shape everything we do, from Sunday worship to how we care for our neighbours.

The good news

Jesus Christ died and rose again so that anyone can be forgiven and made new.

Scripture

The Bible is God's word to us — trustworthy, and the foundation for how we live.

The Church

Following Jesus is not meant to be done alone. We grow best in community.

Mission

God has sent us to love our city — through word, service, and everyday faithfulness.

Our values

Three commitments guide our life together as a church family.

Presence

Authentic worship

We make space to genuinely meet with God, not perform for each other.

Belonging

Real community

Groups and Sunday gatherings exist to help you know and be known.

Generosity

Open hands

We give our time, resources, and homes freely, the way we've received.

Leadership

Meet our pastor

Pastor Ruben Rana Magar

Ruben has led New Hope Church for over a decade, alongside his wife and a team of local elders. His preaching centres on the hope found in Christ, and his door is open to anyone in the congregation who wants to talk, pray, or simply be heard.
