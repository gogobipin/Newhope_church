Generosity is a form of worship

When we give through tithes and offerings from the blessings God has provided, it displays our worship, gratitude, and devotion to Him. Thank you for sowing into all that God is doing at New Hope and across Nepal.

Ways to give

Choose whichever method works best for you — every gift matters.

In person

Sunday offering

Give during either Sunday Gathering — offering boxes are at the back of the hall.

Online

Bank transfer

Give anytime via direct transfer. Account details are available at the welcome desk or by request.

Mobile

eSewa / Khalti

Send a gift instantly through mobile wallet — fast, secure, and simple.

Where your giving goes

Every gift supports the day-to-day life of our church — from Sunday gatherings to the people we serve across Kathmandu.

Sunday Gatherings & teaching

Worship, space, and resources for weekly services.

Community service

Practical support for neighbours in need across the city.

Youth & children's ministry

Teaching, mentoring, and safe spaces for the next generation.

New Hope
